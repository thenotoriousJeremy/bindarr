# Community card art

The images in this directory are community-contributed card scans, bundled with Bindarr for cards that the upstream catalogs have no image for. Each image was submitted through a GitHub issue by its owner, who agreed to share it under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license (CC-BY-4.0).

Files are named `<card id>.png` (max width 745 px). The card artwork itself remains the property of its respective rights holders (The Pokemon Company, Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK, etc.); the contributor's license covers only their scan or photo.
